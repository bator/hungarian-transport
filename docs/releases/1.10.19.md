Hungarian transport 1.10.19. Live delays and positions for the Volán, Volánbusz local and city cards now come from MÁV's own journey planner first.

## Changes

- **mavplusz.hu as the primary realtime source:** for the Volán, Volánbusz local, Paks, Debrecen, Veszprém and Miskolc cards the integration asks MÁV's journey planner (mavplusz.hu) for just the trips the card shows, in one small request a minute, instead of downloading the operator's whole GTFS-Realtime feed (1.9 MB for the Volán coaches). The delays, predicted times, cancellations and vehicle positions are the same data: the jbb.ghsq.de feeds are exported from this planner.
- **jbb.ghsq.de as the backup:** the feeds are read when the planner fails, is over the rate limit, or knows none of the trips asked about, or cannot place a Veszprém or Miskolc row's line and start time on one of its trips, and they stay the only source for the trains (MÁV, GYSEV). The cards show the same rows either way.
- **Veszprém and Miskolc by line and start time:** their rows are matched on the planner the same way as on the feeds: the planner's list of the line's trips that day (fetched once every three hours) gives the trip starting at the row's minute, told apart by the row's stops when both ends of the line start together.
- Sources opt in with `"mavplusz": true` in `realtime_feeds.json`; removing the keys turns the planner off. See `docs/realtime.md` for what is asked, how often, and MÁV's terms.
