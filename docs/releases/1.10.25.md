## Highlights

- **Live delays for Volán timetables without trip ids.** A Volán or Volánbusz town card whose timetable index has no trip ids (one built from a GTFS of your own without them, or before they were kept) now gets its delays and vehicle positions from mavplusz.hu, MÁV's journey planner, by line number and departure time: the line is looked up among the planner's routes of the intercity coaches or of that town's network, and the trip that leaves at the card's minute is asked for. A trip is used only when it calls at your boarding stop and then at your destination, so the other direction of a line leaving at the same minute never takes its place. When the planner does not answer, the card keeps the timetable of your GTFS, as before. Cards whose index has trip ids are asked by them, as before. See [Live delays and positions](../realtime.md#mavpluszhu-first-jbbghsqde-as-backup) (Route name).
