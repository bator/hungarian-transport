Hungarian transport 1.10.23. Includes fresh Volán and city GTFS indexes.

## Highlights

- **MÁV and GYSEV trains from mavplusz.hu first.** Train delays, cancellations and live positions now come from MÁV's own journey planner (mavplusz.hu, OpenTripPlanner), with the jbb.ghsq.de feeds as the backup. A train is answered by the planner only when it knew every trip of that train number; otherwise the row reads the feeds as before. Live positions of trains on a realtime pattern are fetched per route, once per route even when several cards ask at once.
- **BKK boards when FUTÁR fails.** With a FUTÁR key, a board or trip detail FUTÁR cannot give (outage, timeout, HTTP error, FUTÁR's own 429, a rejected key) is answered from mavplusz.hu in FUTÁR's shape, before the card falls back to Transitous. Stations FUTÁR alone knows (the Keleti and Kelenföld presets) are answered from their poles, learnt from FUTÁR and kept across restarts. Not filled from the planner: vehicle positions, alerts, HÉV.
- **Szeged from SZKT's own GTFS.** Trams 2, 3, 3F, 4 and trolleybuses 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 19, 1E from `gtfs.szkt.hu`. Saved tram and trolleybus stops keep working: of the 179 old stop ids, 170 are kept and 6 are aliases of the new stop. Pécs is fetched over https.
- **Timetables between releases from GitHub Pages.** Volán and city timetables are refreshed from the project's GitHub Pages site (option "Weekly Volán and city timetables from GitHub", on by default) instead of a timetable-only release every week. Versions are now only for code.

## Polite use of mavplusz.hu

All planner requests of one Home Assistant share one limit: 30 in a burst and one every two seconds, of which the BKK fallback may use 15 (one every four seconds) and vehicle positions 6 (one every ten seconds). A failing planner is backed off (a minute for the delays, 30 s for the boards, 3 minutes for vehicles), and every request names the integration in its User-Agent. Details: `docs/realtime.md`.

## Upgrade notes

- **Szeged local-bus cards** (`helyi: true`, `city: szeged`): Szeged's buses are only in `volan-szeged` now. Set `city: volan-szeged` and pick the stop again. Such a card shows no buses; if a tram or trolleybus stop has exactly its stop's name, it shows that stop's trams.
- **The tram-train to Hódmezővásárhely** left the Szeged city board: use a MÁV card with its station.
- **Three Szeged tram/trolleybus stops** are not found and have to be picked again: the two Budapesti körút (József Attila sugárút) trolleybus stops and Agyagos utca.
- Nothing to do for MÁV, GYSEV or BKK cards.
