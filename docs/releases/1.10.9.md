## Highlights: timetable sensors

Four new sensors on the *Hungarian transport* device, ready for a dashboard:

- **MÁV route index** — the date of the MÁV timetable the train routes come from, with where it came from (`release`, `daily` from GitHub, or `local`), when it was installed, and the number of trains and lines.
- **Volán timetable** — the date of the Volán timetable, its version, trips and stops, and when it was updated (from a release or by the local updater).
- **City timetables** — when the city timetables were last updated, with the number of operators and trips.
- **Daily MÁV check** — when the daily MÁV index was last checked, the result (installed, up to date, failed, disabled …), the date of GitHub's latest build, the next check and the failure streak.

The sensors update right after a daily or local MÁV install and otherwise every 30 minutes, so the local updater's morning run and a HACS update show up too. The manual has an example entities card.
