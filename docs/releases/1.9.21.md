Hungarian transport 1.9.21. The train map no longer draws another train's route, and a vehicle that has not departed yet is shown waiting at its first stop. Includes fresh Volán, city and MÁV indexes.

## Route map

- **The right route for a MÁV train.** The card finds a train's route in the MÁV index by its train number, and that number is read off BKK's trip id by a rule of thumb; MÁV also reuses numbers. For an Ajka → Budapest-Déli train the number came out as 8714, a Székesfehérvár → Siófok train, and the map drew the Siófok line with the live train far off it near Ajka. Now a pattern that serves your boarding station but neither your alighting station nor the train's destination is not accepted, and the trip's own route from BKK draws instead: Ajka → Veszprém → Székesfehérvár → Kelenföld → Déli, with the train on it.

- **Waiting to depart.** A train or coach that has not left its first station yet is shown there with the dashed marker, and the footer says "Vár az indulásra (menetrend)" / "Waiting to depart (timetable)", on the departure card and the planner alike. Before, nothing was drawn until it departed.
