Hungarian transport 1.10.0. Everything since 1.9.0 in one place: a checked route for every MÁV train, shipped with the release; vehicles without GPS placed by their timetable; a planner that learns your stops; an illustrated manual; and nine review rounds of fixes. Includes fresh Volán, city and MÁV indexes.

## New since 1.9.0

- **A route for every MÁV train, out of the box.** The release ships `mav-index.json.gz`, built from MÁV's GTFS (CC0) and matched to the rails with pfaedle on OpenStreetMap. Every train draws its own route variant (936 sometimes continues to Szombathely), with the boarding and alighting pins on that train's own stations. Every build is checked trip by trip (2,590 trains, 99.9 % of trips on their route). Your own MÁV feed still works: a newer one is built at startup, an upload or the *Rebuild MÁV route index* action always builds it, an older or expired one no longer replaces the release's index.
- **Vehicles without live GPS are on the map.** A MÁV train outside BKK's vehicle feed, a Volán coach or a city bus without GPS is placed by its timetable along the route ("Becsült pozíció (menetrend)"), and a live position replaces it as soon as one arrives.
- **The right train's route.** A train whose number BKK's trip id only suggests (8714 vs the real Ajka → Budapest-Déli train) is no longer drawn on another train's line, neither from the MÁV index nor from Transitous; the trip's own BKK route overrules a route that misses your station.
- **Planner suggestions are your own stops,** ordered by use (older picks fade), stored per Home Assistant user and shared across your devices.
- **City lines draw on the right street:** platforms more than 100 m from their station are their own stops; all 62,607 city trips pass the route check, and every release checks it again.
- **Phone-width rows:** below 440 px each departure row uses two lines, so the destination stays readable.
- **BKK errors in your language,** told apart: a rejected key, the integration's own rate limit and an outage each say what to do.
- **User manual** in English and Hungarian, with screenshots of the real cards.
- **Releases never change after publishing;** a weekly run publishes a new patch only when a timetable really changed (Volán, city or MÁV).

## Fixed in 1.10.0

- **InterCity and other long-distance trains from Transitous were treated as buses.** Transitous reports them as `LONG_DISTANCE`; they are trains again: they fill MÁV boards, show their train number, get the MÁV route on the map and find their live vehicle.
- **"Waiting to depart" only where the train really starts,** and only shortly before it leaves: a train known only from its departure and travel time is no longer drawn waiting at your own stop hours ahead.
- **Volán stops from Transitous** are recognised again under their new ids (`hu-volan-helykozi_…`).
- **Planner outage message:** when every route search fails, the planner says "Az útvonalkeresés most nem érhető el. Próbáld újra." and tries again, instead of "no route for this time".
- **Another train's Transitous trip** can no longer slip back in through the "lone train at the same minute" fallback.
- **Smaller card fixes:** a loop line's estimated position stays on the line; when live GPS is lost the open map falls back to the timetable estimate (or says there is no live GPS) instead of freezing; your last week's picks always stay in the suggestions; a city-prefixed bus-station destination keeps its route filter in the editor; a rate limit on a card-level key keeps the last board for a few minutes; the planner says "indulás legkorábban ma 07:05" instead of a wrong "07:05-tól"; map focus returns to the right button.
- **Integration:** a shipped index of the same feed clears a stale "MÁV build failed" Repairs issue; a Home Assistant crash during a MÁV build counts as an interruption, while an ordinary shutdown does not; BKK's own rate limit (HTTP 429) is reported as a rate limit, and an over-long request as "the request was too long" instead of a BKK outage; leftover temporary index files are cleaned up.
- **Timetable updates:** feeds written with a space after every comma build again; an `http://` Volán override is fetched over HTTPS; untimed intermediate stops stay on their line; a rejected Volán zip or a failed MÁV audit is not downloaded and rebuilt every day, and is reported on the third day; the release audit picks MÁV route variants exactly like the card; shellcheck and actionlint run on every pull request.

## Upgrade notes

- **Restart needed:** restart Home Assistant after updating, then reload the dashboard.
- **Own MÁV feed:** after adding a feed, run *Rebuild MÁV route index* (or upload it); a restart alone builds it only when it is newer than the release's index.
- **Card-level `apiKey` is deprecated** (since 1.9.10): put the BKK key in the integration's settings.
- **Own `scripts/` copy:** copy `scripts/` from this release, not from `main`.

- **Local updater:** a Volán build failure now alerts on the third day in a row, like the city feeds; a failed download still alerts at once.
