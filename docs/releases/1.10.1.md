Hungarian transport 1.10.1. Includes fresh Volán and city GTFS indexes.

## Highlights

- **Operator colour themes for the cards.** A new **Theme** field in the card editor (YAML: `theme`) paints the card's title band in an operator's colours, taken from its own passenger site: BKK purple, MÁV blue with the yellow rule, Volán coach yellow, and ten city operators (MVK Miskolc, SZKT Szeged, Tüke Busz Pécs, V-Busz Veszprém, Blaguss Szombathely, Bahart, Kecskeméti Kisvasút, Thury-Busz Várpalota, HOMM Budaörs, Paksbusz).
- **`operator` picks the theme for you.** It follows the card's own source: a BKK card turns purple, a MÁV card blue, a Volán card or a Volánbusz town network yellow, a city card its operator's colours.
- **Your Home Assistant theme stays in charge of the card body.** Only the title band and the card's accent (row hover, badges of lines without their own colour) change. Background, text, dividers and delay colours keep following your HA theme, so light and dark mode both stay readable. Line badges keep their timetable colours.
- **Nothing changes until you pick one.** The default is `ha`, Home Assistant's own look; existing cards look exactly as before.

```yaml
type: custom:hungarian-transport-card
theme: operator   # or ha, bkk, mav, volan, mvk, szkt, tuke, vbusz, blaguss, bahart, kisvasut, thury, homm, paksbusz
```

## Notes

- **Planner:** under `operator` the planner keeps Home Assistant's look, since it spans every operator; a fixed theme (`bkk`, `mav`, …) applies to it too.
- **Cities without a theme:** T-Busz Tatabánya, KKZRT Kaposvár, HVSZ Hódmezővásárhely, Halasbusz and PTKE Dombóvár keep Home Assistant's look under `operator`: their sites could not be sampled.
- **Switching themes is instant:** it does not reload departures. An unknown theme name falls back to Home Assistant's look.

## Upgrade notes

- **Reload the dashboard** after updating so the browser picks up the new card bundle.
