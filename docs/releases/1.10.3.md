## Fix: "a purple top over a black card looks odd"

- **Cause:** an operator theme (`theme: bkk`, `operator`, …) painted the title as a solid band in the brand colour with white text. On a dark Home Assistant theme that gave a bright purple (or blue, or yellow) lid over a black card body.
- **Now:** the title sits on a light tint of the brand colour (about 15 %) mixed into the card's own background, so it follows your HA theme: a soft tint on light cards, a deep one on dark cards. The title keeps your theme's text colour, and the brand colour shows as a thin rule under it (MÁV keeps its yellow rule).
- **Nothing to change on your side:** cards with `theme` pick up the new look after the update; cards without it stay as they are. Line badges beside the title (`headerBadges`) work with or without a theme.
