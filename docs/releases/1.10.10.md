Hungarian transport 1.10.10. Live delays and vehicle positions outside Budapest, and eight new timetables. Includes fresh Volán and city GTFS indexes.

## Highlights: realtime for Volán, city networks and trains

- **Live delays and positions:** Volán coaches (intercity and the local networks of some 60 towns), DKV Debrecen, Paks, Orosháza, V-Busz Veszprém, MVK Miskolc and MÁV/GYSEV trains now show their live delay on the departure rows and a live vehicle marker ("Élő pozíció") on the route map. Without live data the timetable estimate stays, as before; BKK FUTÁR, ELVIRA and the companion integration keep priority where they already have live data.
- **Source:** the public GTFS-Realtime feeds listed by Transitous and served by jbb.ghsq.de. Home Assistant fetches a feed only while a card needs it, at most about once a minute per feed, with a descriptive User-Agent; nothing runs in the background. A new response-only action, `hungarian_transport.realtime`, serves the cards.
- **Match rates measured on live data:** Volán 97% of running trips, MÁV trains 91%; Veszprém and Miskolc match by line and departure time (Miskolc gives the vehicle's current delay only).

## Eight new timetables

- **City networks:** DKV Debrecen (bus, trolleybus, tram), KeKo Kecskemét, Orosháza and Üröm.
- **Budapest specials:** the Budavári Sikló funicular and the Zugligeti Libegő chairlift, with their own icons.
- **Ferries:** Dunakeszi–Horány and the Pásztor ferry (Szentendre–Szigetmonostor).
- Timetables published as frequencies (every 10 minutes …) are expanded into departures. Every new trip passes the route check.

## Upgrade notes

- **Restart needed:** restart Home Assistant after updating, then reload the dashboard.
- **Data sources:** the Debrecen and Kecskemét timetables are taken from gtfs.menetbrand.com; the funicular, chairlift, ferries and Üröm from gy-mate.hu (CC BY 4.0). See the README credits and `docs/realtime.md`.
