Hungarian transport 1.10.18. A restructuring release: the cards and the integration behave as before, with a few small fixes found on the way.

## Fixes

- **Remembered routes across midnight:** a route pattern the card remembered was checked against the browser's own day, not Budapest's. A dashboard viewed in another time zone could reuse yesterday's pattern after Budapest midnight, or drop today's too early.

- **Same MÁV timetable kept on the update host:** the timetable update script decided which copy of the same MÁV timetable to keep by file size alone. It now uses the integration's rule: a copy drawn along the real rails (pfaedle) is kept over one drawn stop to stop, whatever their sizes.

- **Local MÁV timetable builds:** a MÁV timetable built in Home Assistant now reads the same feeds as the release build. A feed with padded column names came out empty, and one in Windows-1250 encoding failed to build.

## Changes

- The departure board, the ride map and the planner's journey plan each moved into their own module, with tests of their own. Nothing changes on the cards.
- The integration's ELVIRA, FUTÁR and realtime sources share one rate limit and outage log, and a single reader for GTFS feeds, the MÁV index and the response codes.

## Upgrade notes

- **Restart needed:** restart Home Assistant after updating, then reload the dashboard.
- **Rebuilding timetables yourself:** the scripts in `scripts/` now read GTFS with the integration's `gtfs_feed.py`. Copy the scripts of the installed release, or copy `custom_components/hungarian_transport/gtfs_feed.py` next to them (see `docs/timetables.md`).
