Hungarian transport 1.9.22. The train map no longer follows another train's route from Transitous either, and the train's own BKK route is always at hand to overrule it. Includes fresh Volán, city and MÁV indexes.

## Route map

- **The Siófok line is gone for good.** 1.9.21 stopped the MÁV index from drawing the Székesfehérvár → Siófok line for an Ajka → Budapest-Déli train, but the same wrong train number (8714, read off BKK's trip id BKK_87141) still reached Transitous, whose timetable at Székesfehérvár lists the real 8714 leaving for Siófok. A matching number used to win outright, past the time window and the direction check, so the Siófok line came back from there, with the live train far off it near Ajka or Veszprém. Now a Transitous train whose listed stops reach neither your alighting station nor the train's headsign is another train, whatever its number says; the Budapest-Déli train under its own Transitous number is matched instead. A Transitous departure without listed stops still matches as before.

- **The train's own route always wins over a route that misses your station.** For a train with a BKK trip, the card now also decodes that trip's own polyline (the response is already on hand for the stop pins, so it costs nothing). When the Transitous route misses the alighting station by more than three kilometres and the BKK route passes it, the BKK route is drawn. A Transitous route through your station is kept, as before.

## Upgrade notes

- **Restart needed:** restart Home Assistant after updating, then reload the dashboard. If the map still shows an old route, reload the browser page once more so the new card bundle is loaded.
