Hungarian transport 1.10.7. Timetable update notifications, a daily check at the time you choose, and the pfaedle-matched MÁV GTFS on the daily site. Includes fresh Volán and city GTFS indexes.

## Highlights: know when the timetables change

- **Notify about timetable updates** (*Settings → Devices & services → Hungarian transport → Configure*, off by default). When it is on, Home Assistant shows a notification when:
  - the daily MÁV route index installs a newer timetable (with its date and the number of trains and lines);
  - an update of the integration brings new Volán, city or MÁV indexes (which ones, by name);
  - the daily MÁV index has failed three days in a row (once; it goes away after the next success).
- **Your own channel too:** optionally name a service, for example `script.ertesit` or `notify.mobile_app_iphone`; it is called with a title and a message besides the Home Assistant notification.
- **Daily check time:** the daily MÁV index is now checked once a day at the time you set (default 06:00), plus once shortly after startup, instead of every six hours.

## The daily site serves the MÁV GTFS too

- Besides the route index, `https://bator.github.io/hungarian-transport/` now carries the official MÁV GTFS of the day (`mav-gtfs.zip`, CC0) and pfaedle's matched trips, shapes and stop times for it (`mav-pfaedle-overlay.zip`, shapes © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), with their sizes and checksums in the manifest. Anyone who used to run pfaedle for these can download them instead.
- The site is now built at 03:15 UTC.

## Upgrade notes

- **Restart needed:** restart Home Assistant after updating, then reload the dashboard.
- **Notifications are off** until you switch them on.
