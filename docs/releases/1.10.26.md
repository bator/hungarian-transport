## Fix: "Székesfehérvár's local buses never show a delay, and the town is in the list twice"

**Cause:** the city index carried Székesfehérvár's local buses twice: from menetbrand's zip (`szekesfehervar`, "Székesfehérvár — helyi", since September 2026) and from the Volán feed (`volan-szekesfehervar`, "Székesfehérvár", since November 2026), with the same 34 lines and trips. Only the Volán one has the trip ids the live feeds know, so a card on the menetbrand one asked for no delays at all.

- **One Székesfehérvár.** The menetbrand zip left the city index; the town's local buses come from the Volán feed, with live delays and vehicle positions from mavplusz.hu and the jbb.ghsq.de feed.
- **Your cards move over by themselves.** A card saved with the old `szekesfehervar` takes `volan-szekesfehervar` when it loads, and its boarding and alighting stops the Volán feed's stops of the same name within 150 m, else the nearest stop within 100 m (all 424 of the old feed's stops are paired). The title, lines and options stay. The saved YAML changes only when you next edit and save the card.
- **Same look as before.** Székesfehérvár's local cards keep blue line badges and no Volán-yellow header under the "operator" theme, as they had with the menetbrand feed. The other Volánbusz town networks keep the Volán theme.

## Fix: "Veszprém's local cards show no departures at the end of November"

**Cause:** V-Busz published its December timetable early, and the 1.10.25 city index took it in place of the one that still ran: Veszprém had no trips from 28 to 30 November, while its buses were running.

- **A timetable published ahead of time no longer leaves a gap.** When a city feed's new zip starts after the build day, the index keeps the previous release's trips of that operator for the days before it, and the new timetable from its first day on. Rebuilt on the 1.10.25 data: 480, 482 and 852 Veszprém trips on 28, 29 and 30 November (before: none), and December as published.
