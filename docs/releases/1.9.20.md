Hungarian transport 1.9.20. The map shows where a train or coach without live GPS is by its timetable, plus fixes from a third code review and release notes from the repository. Includes fresh Volán, city and MÁV indexes.

## Route map

- **Vehicles without a live position are on the map.** The manual promised a dashed marker for a "position estimated from the timetable", but it was never drawn: a MÁV train outside BKK's vehicle feed, a Volán coach or a city bus without GPS showed its route with no vehicle on it. Now the timetable places it: between the last stop it has left and the next one, as far along that run as the time has gone, measured along the route; standing at a stop while it dwells. The footer says "Becsült pozíció (menetrend)" / "Estimated position (timetable)", and every refresh moves the marker. A live position, when one arrives, replaces it.
- **Where the times come from:** BKK's trip details for FUTÁR trips (MÁV trains with a FUTÁR trip id too, predicted times when given), the shipped GTFS timetable for Volán and city rows.
- **Not covered:** a MÁV train known only from ELVIRA, with no FUTÁR trip, still has no position to estimate from.
