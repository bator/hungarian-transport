Hungarian transport 1.9.20. The route map shows where a train or coach without live GPS is by its timetable; BKK errors are explained in your language; fixes from a third code review; release notes come from the repository. Includes fresh Volán, city and MÁV indexes.

## Route map

- **Vehicles without a live position are on the map.** The manual promised a dashed marker for a "position estimated from the timetable", but it was never drawn: a MÁV train outside BKK's vehicle feed, a Volán coach or a city bus without GPS showed its route with no vehicle on it. Now the timetable places it: between the last stop it has left and the next one, as far along that run as the time has gone, measured along the route; standing on the track beside a station while it dwells. The footer says "Becsült pozíció (menetrend)" / "Estimated position (timetable)", every refresh moves the marker, and a live position, when one arrives, replaces it.
- **Where the times come from:** BKK's trip details for FUTÁR trips (MÁV trains with a FUTÁR trip id too, predicted times when given, shifted by the delay the board shows now); the shipped GTFS timetable for Volán and city rows; and for a train known only from Transitous and ELVIRA, its departure and travel time between your boarding and alighting stops.
- **Gets the hard cases right:** an out-and-back line (the stop is passed twice) is drawn on the right leg; a stop outside the drawn hop is not snapped onto the hop's end; a departed row that has left the board keeps its marker moving.

## Departure card and planner

- **BKK errors in your language, and told apart:** a rejected key says so and what to do ("Kérj újat, és add meg az integráció beállításaiban"); the integration's own request limit says the next refresh tries again; an outage names its detail. Before, the card showed "BKK HTTP 401" or an English sentence whatever the language.
- **Planner:** a failed itinerary search (Transitous or FUTÁR down) says "Az útvonalkeresés most nem érhető el. Próbáld újra." instead of "no route for this time".
- **One word for the look-ahead:** "Előretekintés" in the card editor, the planner and the integration (was "Menetrend előre" and "Meddig"); "irányítópult" for dashboard, as Home Assistant says it; "átszállás nélkül" / "direct" instead of "0 átszállás"; "indulás ma 16:00-tól".

## Manual

- **Removing the API key:** the manual said clearing the field removes the key; it keeps it. The **Remove the stored key** checkbox removes it, and the manual now says so.
- Volán data source is opendata.utas.hu with KTI as fallback; the city list covers 16 operators and the Volánbusz local networks of some 60 towns; the planner editor also has no City field; the timetable-index troubleshooting row points to a reinstall, not Repairs.

## Releases

- **Release notes from the repository:** the Release workflow publishes `docs/releases/<version>.md` as the release description when that file exists on the released commit. A stable release without one stops before anything is built (a weekly timetable release may have taken the number the notes were written for); the weekly timetable release and `-rev` prereleases keep their one-line description. The 1.9.19 notes are kept there as well.
