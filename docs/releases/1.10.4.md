## Highlights: a new look for the planner

- **The route is the title.** Once origin and destination are picked, the planner's title reads `Budapest-Keleti → Szeged`, with a line under it saying what the card shows (`live departures, 3 h` or `leaving today 16:00`). Swap (⇅) and edit (✎) sit beside it. The origin and destination fields fold away; the pencil opens them again, and the tick, a new destination or a swap folds them back.
- **When is one row of pills.** Now, Depart and Arrive, then the look-ahead or the time, in a single row.
- **Itineraries as a striped table.** One row per itinerary on shared columns: departure, lines, transfers, arrival (+1 after midnight) and duration. Click a row and it opens in place with the transfer, walking and waiting summary, the small map and the timeline. On a narrow card the transfer column steps aside and the duration wraps.
- **Same stripes on the live board and in search hits**, readable on light and dark themes alike.

## Fix: the time shows in the card's language

- **Cause:** the time field was the browser's own date-time input, which follows the browser's locale, not the card's. A Hungarian card on an English browser showed `10/31/2026, 09:05 AM`.
- **Now:** the time is picked as a day (`ma`, `holnap`, then weekday and date) and a 24-hour clock in five-minute steps, both in the card's language, for Depart and Arrive. Stepping through the clock with the arrow keys plans once, when you stop.

## Notes

- **Line badges beside the title** (`headerBadges`) belong to the departure card. The planner's title is now the route, so its editor no longer offers the switch.
- **Keyboard:** the swap and edit buttons keep the focus across clicks and live refreshes.
