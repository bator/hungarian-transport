Hungarian transport 1.10.21. A fix release from four rounds of full code review: the journey planner around midnight, city boards filled from Transitous, MÁV boards from ELVIRA under load, live data, the daily MÁV timetable, and Home Assistant's stability.

## Fixes

- **Home Assistant could crash on a broken answer:** a deeply nested JSON answer from ELVIRA, BKK or the daily MÁV timetable could crash the whole Home Assistant process instead of failing that one request. Such answers are now refused before they are read.
- **Planner planned for yesterday after midnight:** with the planner left open past midnight (on a wall tablet), "ma" still meant yesterday, so a time picked after midnight planned the day before. The day picker, the title and the itinerary's day labels now move on at midnight, also when a picker has the focus and when the card comes back into view.
- **Wrong town on city boards:** a city board whose destination has only a name ("Vasútállomás" on a Győr, Eger or Pécs card) could be planned from another town's stop of that name, and showed "No departures". The card's town is now part of the search.
- **City boards emptied by one failed request:** a city board showing only Transitous departures went blank for ten minutes when one Transitous request failed. It now keeps the departures still due, and a failed request is retried after a short pause instead of on every poll.
- **MÁV cards under load:** a dashboard with many MÁV cards could use hundreds of megabytes while ELVIRA's station boards were loading, and make Home Assistant unresponsive for a moment. At most three boards load at a time now, kept small in memory; a card whose board is still waiting says so instead of reporting ELVIRA as down, and loads it on the next poll.
- **Live data after a planner outage:** when mavplusz.hu stopped answering, every poll waited 20 seconds and asked again, without a warning in the log. It now backs off for a minute and logs the outage once. A healthy planner is no longer counted as down when a request starts late in a busy round.
- **Live data from another day's run:** a row could show last night's vehicle for today's trip when only one feed had both runs. Each feed now answers with the same day's run.
- **One failing realtime feed kept being asked:** when one feed failed and another answered, the failing one was never paused and warned on every poll. Feeds are now paused and warned about one by one.
- **Destination lists:** the planner and the card editor now load a destination list again after a BKK outage (with a growing pause, at most six times), without asking BKK for stops it does not know; the editor no longer offers a list loaded for the previous mode or city, which could save BKK lines on a MÁV card.
- **BKK key prompt:** a "BKK key rejected" prompt now closes on its own when BKK accepts the key again, also after a reload, and its repair notice goes with it.
- **Removing the integration** also removes its notifications and its repair notices, and keeps the record of the installed MÁV timetable for when it is added again.
- **Daily MÁV timetable:** the daily build now runs when MÁV's timetable of the day is out, and Home Assistant looks again later in the day while the published one is still yesterday's, so the default 06:00 check no longer always installs the previous day's timetable. A download that does not match its manifest right after an update is taken for the web cache catching up and tried again, not reported as a failure.
- **"Shipped new timetables" for an older timetable:** an update that ships an older Volán or city timetable than the one installed no longer announces new timetables.
- **A card-level `apiKey` of only spaces** no longer counts as a key and no longer hides another card's key.

## Changes

- **Smoother wall tablets:** the Volán and city timetables are read in the background, so the page no longer freezes for up to a second when they load.
- **Fewer requests to Transitous:** answers are kept for two minutes and shared between cards asking the same thing; itineraries that start with a long walk to another stop are no longer shown as departures.
- **Card-level `apiKey`:** a repair notice now lists the dashboards that still have a card-level BKK key. The card-level key is removed in 2.0; set the key in the integration instead.
- The daily MÁV timetable files on GitHub Pages are attested and can be checked with `gh attestation verify` (see docs/timetables.md).
- The weekly timetable release now goes out when one city feed fails to download: that city keeps its previous timetable for the week.

## Upgrade notes

- **Restart needed:** restart Home Assistant after updating, then reload the dashboard.
