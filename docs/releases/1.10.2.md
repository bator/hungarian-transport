Hungarian transport 1.10.2. Includes fresh Volán and city GTFS indexes.

## Highlights

- **Line badges beside the card title.** A new switch in the card editor, *Line badges beside the title* (YAML: `headerBadges: true`), puts the lines on the board next to the card title as badges in their own timetable colours, so you see at a glance what a card serves: `70 133E` on a Csömöri út → Keleti card, `S36 IC G43` on a Kelenföld → Székesfehérvár card.
- **Compact on every width.** Each line shows once, in departure order, four at most, then a neutral `+N`. On a narrow card the title shortens with `…` and the badges stay in place. An empty board shows the plain title.
- **Off by default.** Existing cards look exactly as before until you turn it on. Toggling it does not reload departures.

```yaml
type: custom:hungarian-transport-card
headerBadges: true
```
