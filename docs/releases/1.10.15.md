Hungarian transport 1.10.15. Fixes from a full review of the code, most of them for the MÁV cards changed in 1.10.13.

## Fixes

- **MÁV cards when ELVIRA fails:** a failed, slow or refused answer from MÁV ELVIRA no longer empties a MÁV card. The trains still due stay on the board under an error line, and one bad moment no longer pauses every MÁV card for three minutes.
- **Train kinds and termini:** a fast train (sebesvonat) shows as SV, not as a stopping train (Sz), and each row shows where the train goes, not the card's destination. (The 1.10.13 notes said "S"; the badge is SV.)
- **Planner "Now":** trains missing from BKK FUTÁR (IC TÓPART, 247 CITADELLA) now appear on the planner's board too, it refreshes every five minutes while it is on screen, and a timed search from a city stop finds the trains of the planned time.
- **MÁV cards without a BKK key:** they come back on their own after a Home Assistant restart, and say clearly when a destination has no MÁV station code.
- **Maps after a timetable update:** an open dashboard keeps drawing the routes of the timetable it loaded, instead of straight lines until midnight.
- **Live data:** MÁV trains keep their live delays when a weekly timetable download fails once; Miskolc trips without a vehicle show no made-up delay of 0.
- **Ferries, the Sikló and the Libegő** from Transitous show as such, not as buses.

## Changes

- **Fewer BKK requests:** a card checks which trips reach its destination from the route data it already has, and asks for each trip separately only when that cannot tell. Dashboards with BKK cards make fewer requests when they load and poll.
- **Home Assistant memory:** expired MÁV and BKK answers are released when they expire.
- **`elvira_between` service:** new optional `at` field (the start of the window), `dest_name` limited to 100 characters, a `dest_error` field when a destination board is missing, and each departure carries its terminus (`end`).

## Upgrade notes

- **Restart needed:** restart Home Assistant after updating, then reload the dashboard.
