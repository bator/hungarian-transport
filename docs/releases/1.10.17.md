Hungarian transport 1.10.17. The cards load even when a dashboard is opened while Home Assistant is starting.

## Fixes

- **"Configuration error" after a restart:** a dashboard opened or reloaded while Home Assistant was still starting (the automatic reload after an update, for example) showed "Configuration error" on every card until the page was reloaded again. The integration now also keeps the cards as a Lovelace resource, which such a page still loads.

## Upgrade notes

- **Restart needed:** restart Home Assistant after updating, then reload the dashboard. A `/hungarian-transport/` entry appears under Settings → Dashboards → Resources; it is managed by the integration. With resources in YAML mode nothing is added: add `/hungarian-transport/hungarian-transport-loader.js` as a module yourself.
