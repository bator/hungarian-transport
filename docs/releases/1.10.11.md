Hungarian transport 1.10.11. Fixes for the live delays introduced in 1.10.10.

## Fixes

- **Ride time:** with a live delay at both the boarding and the destination stop, the ride time on a row could move by the difference twice (e.g. 8 minutes instead of 4). It now moves once, from the timetable's ride time.
- **Several cards from one stop:** two cards from the same stop towards different destinations no longer share one live answer, so each shows the delay at its own destination.
- **MÁV and GYSEV:** an unreadable train timetable download from the live-data source is now treated as an outage that is retried after 15 minutes. Before, it made the whole live-data call fail and paused live data on every card for 5 minutes.

## Upgrade notes

- **Restart needed:** restart Home Assistant after updating, then reload the dashboard.
