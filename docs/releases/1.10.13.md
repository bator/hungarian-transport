Hungarian transport 1.10.13. MÁV cards now list their trains from MÁV's own departure data.

## Fixes

- **Missing MÁV trains:** a MÁV card took its trains from BKK FUTÁR, whose timetable lacks some of them: from Székesfehérvár towards Kelenföld every IC TÓPART (07:37, 09:37, ...) and the 247 CITADELLA. The card now lists MÁV ELVIRA's departures from the boarding station, for the trains that also reach the destination.
- **Late evening trains:** ELVIRA's boards for the busiest stations (Kelenföld) were cut at about 22:30, and arrivals past midnight were not looked up. Both are now read in full.

## Changes

- **MÁV cards and BKK:** a MÁV card's departures need no BKK key or FUTÁR request when the integration is installed. If ELVIRA does not answer, the trains come from Transitous until it does.
- **Train names:** rows show ELVIRA's names (TÓPART instead of IC). A train with only a number shows its kind (S, Sz, IC).
- **Line filter:** the lines saved on a MÁV card no longer filter its trains. Every train from the station to the destination is listed.

- **Restart needed:** restart Home Assistant after updating, then reload the dashboard.
